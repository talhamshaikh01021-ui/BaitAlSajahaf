The House

Made slowly, worn for a lifetime.

It began with a single bottle of aged oud and a patient distiller.

We still work that way. Each extrait is blended in small batches, rested for ninety days and filtered by hand. The house draws on the old attar traditions of the subcontinent and the Gulf, then lets modern perfumery clear the air around them.

Nothing is rushed. Nothing is loud. A fragrance should arrive a moment after you do, and stay a moment after you leave.
